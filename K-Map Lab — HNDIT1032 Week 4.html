What is a Karnaugh map?

A K-map is a truth table redrawn as a grid. Neighbouring cells differ by exactly one variable, so when you circle neighbouring 1s, that variable cancels out (XY + XY' = X). It replaces awkward Boolean algebra with a picture.

Anatomy of a map

Hover or tap any cell. Each cell is one row of the truth table (one minterm). n variables give 2n cells.

Why the labels go 00, 01, 11, 10 (Gray code)

Tap a cell. Its true neighbours (minterms that differ by one bit) light up yellow. In Gray order they always sit next to it, counting wrap-around from the left edge to the right edge. Switch to binary order and see the adjacency break.

Reading a group in one sentence

Look along the group. A variable that is always 1 stays as itself (A). A variable that is always 0 stays complemented (A). A variable that changes is dropped. Then OR the group terms together.
